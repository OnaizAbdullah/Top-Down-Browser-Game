Game Guide

How to Play

Overview of Game

Orb Collector is a very fun but challenging game to complete; The player is a cell and must eat (collect) all the orbs while avoiding infection by any of the enemies (virions). Depending on selected mode, a new virion will appear on the screen after a certain amount of orbs are consumed. After every orb consumed, the cell will grow in size making the game become gradually harder as the game goes on. There are also shields in the game. If you consume a shield, you are immune to enemies only for about six seconds... so plan well!

Feel free to pause the game at any time by pressing "p" on your keyboard. To unpause, simply press "p" again. Eat 150 orbs to finish the game!

Levels

There are three levels to this game. To finish a level, you must eat 50 orbs. The cell and the virions both increase their speed after every new level. This leads the game to become gradually harder after every level. If you manage to finish all three levels of the game, you will be put on an elite leader-board where other people can see what difficulty you completed the game at.

Modes

This game consists of three modes:

  • Easy
    1. Enemies will spawn after every five orbs consumed.
    2. Users are allowed to touch the border.
  • Medium
    1. Enemies will spawn after every seven orbs consumed.
    2. Users are allowed to touch the border.
  • Hard
    1. Enemies will spawn after every ten orbs consumed.
    2. Users will die if they touch the border.

Cheats

Some might find this game hard to complete or may not have the time to play through all of it without help.

Fortunately, for those people, there are some cheat codes in the game which makes it way easier to finish (shh don't tell people you used the cheats):

  • Fixed Size Cheat (Pressing "K") - makes the cell stay a fixed size for the whole game. It can be toggled on/off at any point during the game.

  • Double Points Cheat (Pressing "T") - Enables each orb to be worth two points. Note: This cheat can only be enabled at the start of a level of the game.
